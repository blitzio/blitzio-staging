blitzio, imagery source and processing

Surface imagery: NASA Earth Observatory, Blue Marble: Next Generation,
July 2004, base map with topographic shading.
Blue Marble imagery by Reto Stockli and Robert Simmon, NASA/GSFC.

Collection:
https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/blue-marble-next-generation/base-topography/

Original image:
https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/esd/eo/images/bmng/bmng-topography/july/world.topo.200407.3x21600x10800.jpg

NASA media usage guidelines:
https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/

This personal webpage uses NASA-published geographic imagery.
NASA permits personal webpage use under its media guidelines, with source
acknowledgement. NASA logos and endorsement are not used or implied.
No separately identified third-party copyright notice was found on the
selected NASA image source. This is not a blanket license for all NASA media.

Processing by blitzio: spherical reprojection into a 3072px cap texture,
WebP encoding, and dark blue/indigo colour treatment in a Three.js shader.
The visible region is oriented toward maritime Southeast Asia. The approximate
Singapore region is near the centre of the visible cap at a neutral pointer.
There is no marker, label, or location pin in the rendered page.
The source geography has not been procedurally regenerated.

The rendered atmosphere, illumination, and perspective are artistic
approximations. The surface mosaic is historical, not live observation.
No synthetic cloud layer or current-weather claim is included.
The separate Blue Marble cloud archive was investigated but could not be
downloaded from its legacy NASA endpoint during this local session.

Night lights: NASA Earth Observatory,
Black Marble 2016, grayscale 3 km global map.
Black Marble imagery by Joshua Stevens, NASA Earth Observatory, using Suomi
NPP VIIRS data from Miguel Roman, NASA/GSFC.

Collection:
https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/earth-at-night/maps

Original image:
https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/esd/eo/images/imagerecords/144000/144897/BlackMarble_2016_3km_gray.jpg

Processing by blitzio: the same spherical reprojection as the surface imagery
into a 3072px cap texture, WebP encoding, and shown only on the night side of
the rendered day/night line, in a cool lavender white chosen for the site. The lights are a
2016 composite, not a live observation.

Originals are retained outside the served site in .local/nasa-originals/.
Only optimised local textures and two rendered stills (the resting pose and
the night pose the arrival starts from) are served.
