blitzio, imagery source and processing

Surface imagery: NASA Earth Observatory, Blue Marble: Next Generation,
July 2004, base map with topographic shading.
Blue Marble imagery by Reto Stockli and Robert Simmon, NASA/GSFC.

Collection:
https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/blue-marble-next-generation/base-topography/

Original image:
https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/esd/eo/images/bmng/bmng-topography/july/world.topo.200407.3x21600x10800.jpg

Processing by blitzio: spherical reprojection into a 3072px cap texture,
WebP encoding, and dark blue/indigo colour treatment in a Three.js shader.
The visible region is oriented toward maritime Southeast Asia. The approximate
Singapore region is near the centre of the visible cap at a neutral pointer.
There is no marker, label, or location pin in the rendered page.
The source geography has not been procedurally regenerated.

The rendered atmosphere, illumination, and perspective are artistic
approximations. The surface mosaic is historical, not live observation.
No synthetic cloud layer or current-weather claim is included.
The separate Blue Marble cloud archive was investigated but could not be
downloaded from its legacy NASA endpoint during this local session.

Originals are retained outside the served site in .local/nasa-originals/.
Only optimised local textures and a rendered static fallback are served.
